Why CareerForges

Privacy isn't
a feature. It's
the foundation.

Most AI career tools rely on cloud infrastructure and harvest your data. CareerForges takes a radically different approach.

  • ✓ AI runs entirely on your local machine using Ollama or Claude CLI — no API calls to external servers.
  • ✓ Your resume data never leaves your device. Zero cloud storage, zero vendor lock-in.
  • ✓ Open source under a transparent license — audit every line of code yourself.
  • ✓ Built for job seekers worldwide, regardless of geography or economic access.
  • ✓ Community-driven roadmap — your feature requests shape the product.
